# UX SPECIFICATION

## AI-HUMAN-CONNECTION MVP

A simple, privacy-first and safety-first user experience for meaningful dating connections.

---

## 1. UX Goals

The MVP UX should:

- Make registration simple and trustworthy.
- Help users create an authentic profile.
- Clearly explain how AI is used.
- Give users control over their decisions.
- Make compatibility easier to understand.
- Reduce pressure from traditional swipe-based dating.
- Provide safe communication between matched users.
- Make privacy and consent easy to understand.
- Work well on mobile devices.
- Be accessible to users with different abilities.

---

## 2. UX Principles

### 2.1 Human First
AI assists users but never makes relationship decisions for them.

### 2.2 Consent First
Users must understand and approve important actions before they happen.

### 2.3 Privacy First
Users control what information is visible and how their information is used.

### 2.4 Safety First
Safety controls should be available throughout the experience.

### 2.5 Explainable AI
Users should understand why an AI recommendation was made.

### 2.6 Quality Over Quantity
The interface should encourage meaningful connections rather than endless browsing.

### 2.7 Accessibility
The application should support keyboard navigation, screen readers, readable text and accessible controls.
